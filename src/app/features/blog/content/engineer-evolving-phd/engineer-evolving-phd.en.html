🦅 Rising from the Ashes

The engineer may be gone, but like a phoenix rising from the ashes 🦅✨, I'm thrilled to announce that I'm back at the INRIA Lille laboratories—but this time, with a brand new mission!

"Every ending is just a new beginning in disguise. The engineer didn't disappear—he evolved."

🏠 Same Home, New Mission

Here's the beautiful part: my new boss is the University of Lille, but my office is still with the incredible Spirals team at Inria. Some things are just too good to leave! 💙

This means I get the best of both worlds:

  • The academic rigor and prestige of University of Lille
  • The cutting-edge research environment of Inria
  • The amazing colleagues I've grown to love at Spirals
  • Access to world-class resources and expertise

🚀 What's Coming Next?

Get ready for a new chapter filled with:

🧠

Coordination Models

Deep dives into how distributed components communicate and synchronize in complex systems.

🌐

Distributed Systems

Exploring the challenges and solutions for systems that span multiple machines and locations.

⚡

Self-Adaptation

Building systems that can autonomously adjust to changing conditions—like nature's best survivors!

☁️

Cloud Computing

Unleashing the power of scalable, on-demand computing resources for modern applications.
