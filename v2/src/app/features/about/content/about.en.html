Who am I?

"The courage to begin, the determination to persevere, the nobility in action."

I am Brice Arléon ZEMTSOP NDADJI, a passionate software engineer and computer science researcher. My first name "Arléon" embodies the values that define me: the courage of the lion (Leo), the determination to face challenges, and nobility in every action.

Originally from Cameroon, I am currently pursuing a PhD at Université de Lille within Inria's research labs, where I design self-adaptive systems for the cloud. This duality between academic research and industrial expertise is at the heart of my professional identity.

With over 6 years of experience in software development, I have had the opportunity to lead teams, deliver more than 20 projects, and found the Soft Development Community to promote engineering best practices.
